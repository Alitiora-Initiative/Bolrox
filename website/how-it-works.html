How it works

A disciplined path from raw data to a decision — including the decision to stay flat.

  1. 1

    Fetch and cache

    OHLCV from Yahoo with Stooq fallback. Macro yields, COT, and calendar where available. SQLite cache enforces freshness by interval.

  2. 2

    Compute indicators

    EMAs, ATR, RSI, swing structure, and regime proxies calculated in pure code. No guessed values.

  3. 3

    Score confluence

    Higher-timeframe bias, location, momentum, intermarket and macro, events, and positioning combine into a 0–100 score and grade.

  4. 4

    Construct the trade

    Only if grade and risk-reward pass. Entry, stop, TP1–3, suggested size from risk %, and invalidation language.

  5. 5

    Write artifacts

    Markdown report and signals.json under out/YYYY-MM-DD_SYMBOL/, timestamped for auditability.

Non-negotiable: Never fabricate data. Risk before reward. No-trade is a valid outcome. Probabilities remain conservative.
